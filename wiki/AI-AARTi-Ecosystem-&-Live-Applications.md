# The AI-AARTi Ecosystem & Live Web Applications Directory

The **AI-AARTi Open Science Ecosystem** integrates machine-readable research repositories, multi-objective decision engines, and high-resolution climate GIS suites.

---

## 🌐 Live Web Application Index & Authority Links

### 1. [Primary Research Archive (publications.ai-aarti.com)](https://publications.ai-aarti.com)
* **URL**: [https://publications.ai-aarti.com](https://publications.ai-aarti.com)
* **Description**: Central open-access repository featuring peer-reviewed monographs, KaTeX mathematical proofs, AI Scholarly Co-Pilot, RIS/BibTeX export engines, and Highwire Press metadata.

### 2. [Pareto Governance Engine (governanceapp.ai-aarti.com)](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com)
* **URL**: [https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com)
* **Description**: Interactive democratic policy simulator allowing users to test multi-objective compromise functions $U_g(a)$, allocate quadratic voice tokens $C(v) = v^2$, and execute 10,000 Monte Carlo delivery risk simulations.

### 3. [Urban Heat Democratization Engine (urban-heat.ai-aarti.com)](https://urban-heat.ai-aarti.com)
* **URL**: [https://urban-heat.ai-aarti.com](https://urban-heat.ai-aarti.com)
* **Description**: Microclimate GIS explorer featuring 3D Google Earth spatial layers, spectral graph Cheeger cut bottleneck detectors, GMRF satellite temperature downscaling, and CDC SVI equity overlays.

### 4. [Main Academic Portfolio (ai-aarti.com)](https://ai-aarti.com)
* **URL**: [https://ai-aarti.com](https://ai-aarti.com)
* **Description**: Institutional portal featuring executive research briefs, 10-language community action toolkits, top-10 journal benchmark comparisons, and academic dispatch subscriptions.
