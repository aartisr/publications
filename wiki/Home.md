## 📚 Featured Monograph Deep-Dives

### 1. [Pareto-Optimal Governance Engines](./Pareto-Optimal-Governance-Engine.md)
* **Direct Monograph Link**: [Read Full Monograph on Primary Archive](https://publications.ai-aarti.com/pareto-governance-engine)
* **Live Interactive Engine**: [Launch Pareto Engine](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com)
* **Abstract**: Formulates legislative proposal evaluation as a multi-objective Pareto optimization over utility vectors $U_g(a) \in [0, 1]$. Integrates Democratic Voice Token economics $C(v) = v^2$, Rawlsian minimum faction floor protections $U_{\min}$, and stochastic Monte Carlo risk propagation.

### 2. [Democratizing Urban Heat Resilience](./Democratizing-Urban-Heat-Resilience.md)
* **Direct Monograph Link**: [Read Full Monograph on Primary Archive](https://publications.ai-aarti.com/urban-heat-democratization)
* **Live Microclimate Simulator**: [Launch Urban Heat Engine](https://urban-heat.ai-aarti.com)
* **Abstract**: Pairs USGS Landsat-9 TIRS-2 and NASA ECOSTRESS satellite radiometry with the CDC Social Vulnerability Index (SVI). Employs sparse Gaussian Markov Random Fields (GMRF) and normalized graph Laplacians to downscale surface temperatures to sub-15m resolution.

### 3. [Spectral Graph Theory & Cheeger Inequalities](./Spectral-Graph-Theory-%26-Cheeger-Inequalities.md)
* **Direct Monograph Link**: [View Mathematical Proofs](https://publications.ai-aarti.com/pareto-governance-engine#section-2)
* **Abstract**: Details the normalized graph Laplacian $\mathcal{L} = D^{-1/2} L D^{-1/2}$ and Fiedler vector sweeps ($\lambda_2$) bounding the Cheeger conductance $h(G)$, pinpointing thermal bottlenecks in polynomial time $O(|V| \log |V| + |E|)$.

### 4. [Gaussian Markov Random Fields & Satellite Radiometry](./Gaussian-Markov-Random-Fields-%26-Satellite-Radiometry.md)
* **Direct Monograph Link**: [View Satellite Methods](https://publications.ai-aarti.com/urban-heat-democratization#section-3)
* **Abstract**: Spatial Bayesian regularization parameterizing precision matrices $Q$ via 10m Sentinel-2 optical texture and LiDAR surface elevations, solved via sparse Cholesky factorization.
